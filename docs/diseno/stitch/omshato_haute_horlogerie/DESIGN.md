## Brand & Style

This design system expresses the serene, uncompromising discipline of an ultra-exclusive single-seller watch boutique based in Colombia. The brand identity fuses high-horology reverence with minimalist architectural restraint. It evokes the quiet, meditative stillness of a museum vault combined with modern gallery curation—where timepieces are treated not merely as commodities, but as kinetic sculptures.

The aesthetic philosophy is rooted in **Monochrome Architectural Minimalism** and **High-Contrast Editorial Precision**:
- **Tone:** Calm, elevated, contemplative, monolithic, uncompromising.
- **Audience:** Discerning connoisseurs, ultra-high-net-worth collectors, and patrons seeking provenance, bespoke curation, and solitary craftsmanship.
- **Visual Tenets:** Deep obsidian backgrounds, razor-thin luminous divisions, stark monochromatic tension, and deliberate spatial cadence. The complete absence of chromatic saturation directs all sensorial focus to metal finishes, complications, and structural proportions.

## Colors

The palette is strictly achromatic, employing nuanced luminance steps to establish depth, materiality, and tension without pigment.

### Palette Architecture
- **Base Canvas (`#0F0F0F`):** The primary void. An absorbing charcoal black that prevents harsh OLED clipping while eliminating environmental distraction.
- **Secondary Canvas (`#141414`):** Recessed canvas tone for full-bleed section alternating and underlay viewports.
- **Surface Card (`#1A1A1A`):** The standard containment plane for product showcases, case specifications, and conversational modules.
- **Elevated Surface (`#222222`):** Floating sheets, flyout navigation, modal overlays, and transient inspection drawers.
- **Hairline Structural Borders (`#2A2A2A`):** 1px structural boundaries defining the layout grid with architectural precision.
- **Dividers & Separators (`#333333`):** Subtle internal demarcation within component assemblies.
- **Typography & Glyphs:**
  - *Primary (`#FFFFFF`):* High-luminance white for model names, key metrics, and decisive actions.
  - *Secondary (`#9E9E9E`):* Calibrated mid-neutral for movement metadata, caliber specs, and editorial excerpts.
  - *Tertiary (`#666666`):* Subdued neutral for legal footnotes, serial prefixes, and inactive control parameters.

Strict adherence to absolute monochrome is mandatory. No status indicators, badges, or notification accents may introduce chromatic hue; all interactive feedback is communicated through luminance shifts, contrast inversions, and border sharpness.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs the sculptural authority of **Syne** with the pristine legibility of **Plus Jakarta Sans**.

### Rules & Formatting
- **Tracking & Case Hierarchy:** All display headers, collection banners, model classifications, and primary calls-to-action rendered in `Syne` or uppercase `Plus Jakarta Sans` must enforce aggressive tracking (`0.15em` to `0.25em`). This manifests an airy, gallery-grade presence.
- **Body & Editorial Content:** Flowing narratives, provenance chronicles, and caliber specifications utilize `Plus Jakarta Sans` at Light (`300`) or Regular (`400`) weights with generous line-height (`1.6`–`1.75`) to preserve breathability.
- **Numbers & Monetary Expressions:** Chronograph references, dimensions (e.g., `41mm`), reference codes, and valuations must use tabular figures when placed in data tables or specification ribbons to preserve vertical alignment.

## Layout & Spacing

The spatial engine enforces deliberate containment and ceremonial pacing. Layouts prioritize negative space over information packing, framing individual timepieces as solitary works of art.

### Grid Foundations
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column modular grid with generous `gutter-desktop` (2.5rem) and expansive horizontal padding (`margin-desktop` of 5rem). Key editorial layouts max out at 1440px wide to preserve proportional elegance on widescreen displays.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8-column grid with a fluid `margin-tablet` (3rem) and 1.5rem gutters. Product side-by-side matrices convert to single prominent focal views with vertical specs.
- **Mobile (Below 768px):** 4-column structured stack. Section margins tighten to 1.5rem, preserving edge-to-edge full-bleed presentation for macro dial photography.

### Cadence
- Content sections are spaced rhythmically using multiples of `space-xl` (`3.5rem` to `7rem`), ensuring clear separation between distinct timepieces, technical dossiers, and acquisition protocols.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids soft, organic drop shadows or diffuse blur aesthetics. Instead, depth is conveyed through **disciplined tonal layering and sharp luminous hairlines**.

### Surface Elevation Tiers
1. **Base Plane (`#0F0F0F`):** The primary foundation. Contains hero narratives, brand manifesto passages, and atmospheric background layers.
2. **Structural Plane (`#1A1A1A`):** Elevated cards, spec compartments, and timepiece display plinths. Always delineated from the base plane by a crisp `1px` border in `#2A2A2A`.
3. **Overlaid Architecture (`#222222`):** Flyout acquisition menus, private concierge interfaces, and high-zoom inspection drawers. Surrounded by a `1px` border in `#333333` with zero drop-shadow bleed, preserving flat, razor-sharp geometric edges.

### Optical Highlights & Glass
When layered image overlays occur (e.g., dial detail magnifying viewports), utilize purely neutral backdrop filters (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`) backed by `rgba(15, 15, 15, 0.85)` with a top edge illuminated by a single-pixel gradient hairline fading from `#FFFFFF` (15% opacity) to `#FFFFFF` (0% opacity).

## Shapes

The design system is fundamentally **monolithic, architectural, and sharp**. 

### Geometry Rules
- **Corner Curvature:** Strict 0px radius across all structural cards, banners, image viewports, and interactive containers.
- **Exceptions:** A minute, imperceptible `2px` border-radius is permitted exclusively on primary and secondary action buttons to prevent visual distortion against dark backgrounds while maintaining a clean, chiseled silhouette.
- **Edge Integrity:** Surfaces intersect with absolute orthogonal precision. No pill shapes, circles, or organic contours exist within the layout framework, reflecting the exacting tolerances of Swiss movement manufacture and modern architecture.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Solid white background (`#FFFFFF`), pure black text (`#0F0F0F`), 2px border radius. Typography: `label-lg`, uppercase, `0.20em` letter-spacing. Padding: `14px 32px`. Hover state shifts background to `#D4D4D4` with instant transition.
- **Secondary Action:** Transparent background, hairline border (`1px solid #FFFFFF`), pure white text (`#FFFFFF`), 2px border radius. Padding: `13px 31px`. Hover state inverts to solid white background with black text.
- **Tertiary / Text Action:** Transparent background, no border. Pure white text with an understated `1px solid #666666` underline offset by 6px. Hover state brightens the underline to `#FFFFFF`.

### Cards & Timepiece Showcases
- Background in Surface Card (`#1A1A1A`) framed with a continuous `1px solid #2A2A2A` hairline border.
- Generous internal padding (`space-lg` to `space-xl`).
- High-resolution watch imagery sits against an absolute black or dark neutral backdrop, avoiding ambient drop shadows.
- Hover interactions feature an imperceptible border shift from `#2A2A2A` to `#666666` with a slow 400ms ease.

### Input Fields & Inquiries
- Base background: `#141414`.
- Border: `1px solid #2A2A2A`, 0px border radius.
- Typography: `body-md` in `#FFFFFF`, placeholder text in `#666666`.
- Focus state: Border transitions to `#FFFFFF` with no glow or outer halo. A subtle 1px white underline indicator remains active.
- Padding: `16px 20px`.

### Chips & Specification Tags
- Rectangular tags (0px radius) with `#1A1A1A` background and `1px solid #333333` hairline.
- Typography: `label-sm`, uppercase, `#9E9E9E`.
- Active / Selected State: Border in `#FFFFFF`, label text in `#FFFFFF`.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Square profiles (0px radius) measuring 16x16px with `1px solid #666666`.
- Checkbox selected state: Solid `#FFFFFF` fill with an interior `#0F0F0F` micro-check mark.
- Radio selected state: Solid `#0F0F0F` interior with an inner 6x6px solid `#FFFFFF` square.

### Horological Specification Matrices (Bespoke Component)
- Multi-row key-value table designed for movement caliber, power reserve, case diameter, water resistance, and crystal material.
- Rows separated by `1px solid #2A2A2A` dividers.
- Key column rendered in `label-md` uppercase (`#666666`), value column rendered in `body-md` (`#FFFFFF`).

### Provenance & Serial Seals (Bespoke Component)
- Minimalist monolithic badge verifying authenticity and single-seller provenance in Colombia.
- Framed in a double-hairline border (`#2A2A2A` outer, `#333333` inner with 2px gap).
- Monospaced serial metrics set in uppercase tabular styling.
